Things worth finding. Independently recommended, not sources for specific essays.

- [Reticulum Network Stack](https://reticulum.network): cryptography-based networking for local-first and off-grid communication. Works over LoRa, serial, TCP, I2P, any medium.
- [Aral Balkan: The Small Web](https://ar.al/2020/08/07/what-is-the-small-web/): essay on building a web that serves people instead of corporations. The case for personal sites, small servers, and human-scale technology.
- [Guifi.net](https://guifi.net): one of the largest community networks in the world. 35,000+ nodes in Catalonia, built and run by its users.
- [permacomputing.net](https://permacomputing.net): wiki documenting the permacomputing movement. Principles, practices, and community resources.
- [LOW←TECH MAGAZINE](https://solar.lowtechmagazine.com): a solar-powered website about low-tech solutions. The original inspiration for running a site on sunlight.
