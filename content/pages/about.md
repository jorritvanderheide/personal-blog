I am Jorrit van der Heide. I work in technology and think about what it means
to build systems that are durable, understandable, and owned by the people who
use them.

This site is a place for essays and notes on the topics I keep returning to:
digital rights and privacy, NixOS and declarative infrastructure, self-hosting,
permacomputing, community networks, and the small web. Sometimes I write about
gardening.

You can reach me through the channels listed on my Codeberg profile.
